# Definitive Master Policy

Status: **Locked**  
Scope: permanent project architecture

## Purpose

The vehicle visible in the MVP is a CSS-based interaction prototype. It exists only to validate the experience of moving time.

It is not a production vehicle asset. It must never be refined, reused, or represented as the production visual asset.

The production asset is called the **Definitive Master**.

## Definitive Master identity

| Field | Locked value |
|---|---|
| Vehicle | Toyota VOXY |
| Generation | Fourth generation / 90 Series |
| Grade | S-Z Hybrid 2WD |
| Model year | 2022–Present |

This identity is the permanent reference vehicle for the project. Do not mix parts from another grade, variant, or generation.

## Geometry policy

Production geometry may be derived only from:

1. Official Toyota dimensions
2. Official Toyota press photography
3. Official Toyota catalogs
4. Verified real-vehicle inspection
5. Direct measurements, when available

AI-generated images must never be used as geometric references. They may be used only for concept exploration or moodboards.

The Definitive Master must be a purpose-built, high-fidelity 3D surface model.

## Asset separation

### MVP prototype assets

- Purpose: validate interaction, rhythm, and the experience of moving time
- Current form: CSS vehicle silhouette and interface effects
- Permitted use: prototypes, internal interaction testing
- Prohibited use: production imagery, geometric reference, vehicle comparison, morph source

### Definitive Master assets

- Purpose: the geometric foundation of the product
- Form: accurate 3D surface model and controlled render set
- Required for: temporal evolution, generation comparison, morphing, animation, and production interaction
- Requirement: every future vehicle asset follows this same production standard

## Quality gate

Hide the year, generation, badges, specifications, labels, and explanatory text.

If the vehicle's shape alone communicates the passage of time, the asset succeeds.

If explanation is required, the asset fails.

Accuracy precedes aesthetics. No amount of visual beauty can compensate for inaccurate geometry.
